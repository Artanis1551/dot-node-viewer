# ADR 0006: Frozen v1 Unified Graph IR & Format Abstraction Contract

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Stage:** S01

## Context

Stages S02 through S10 introduce format detectors and streaming parsers for five neural network ecosystems (ONNX, TensorFlow Lite, ExecuTorch, PyTorch TorchScript/`.pt2`, and Keras `.keras`/`.h5`), while Stage S04 introduces the Sugiyama layered layout engine (`DotNodeViewer.Layout`) and Stages S05–S11 build the Avalonia UI (`DotNodeViewer.App`).

Without a frozen contract between format parsers, layout, CLI tooling, and the UI, adding new model formats would risk cascading breaking changes across downstream projects and invalidating golden topology snapshots (`*.expected.json`).

## Decision

1. **Freeze the v1 Unified Graph IR (`DotNodeViewer.Core.Ir`):**
   The public record hierarchy in `DotNodeViewer.Core.Ir` is frozen as the v1 IR contract:
   - **Graph Container & Metadata:** `ModelGraph`, `ModelFormatInfo`, `ModelFormatId`, `Subgraph`, `ParseDiagnostic`, `DiagnosticSeverity`.
   - **Nodes & Operators:** `GraphNode`, `OperatorInfo`, `OperatorCategory`, `NodePort`, `GraphPort`, `NodeAttribute`, and the 13-case closed discriminated union `AttributeValue` (`Int64`, `Float64`, `Bool`, `String`, `Int64List`, `Float64List`, `StringList`, `DataTypeValue`, `ShapeValue`, `Tensor`, `Graph`, `GraphList`, `Bytes`).
   - **Tensors, Shapes & Quantization:** `TensorEdge`, `TensorKind`, `TensorDescriptor`, `DataType`, `TensorShape`, the 3-case closed union `TensorDimension` (`Fixed`, `Symbolic`, `Dynamic`), and the closed union `QuantizationInfo` (`AffineQuantization`, `BlockQuantization`).
   - **Lazy Weight Storage:** The closed union `TensorDataSource` (`TensorDataSlice` with `TensorDataEncoding`, and `ExternalTensorDataReference`).
2. **Freeze the Public Parser & Inspection Abstractions (`DotNodeViewer.Core.Abstractions`):**
   - `IModelFormatDetector` and `IModelFormatReader` define the contract for format sniffing and streaming graph extraction.
   - `ModelProbe` guarantees non-destructive 64-byte header inspection and lazy ZIP central-directory listing while restoring `Stream.Position`.
   - `ModelFormatRegistry` provides explicit, reflection-free registration and lookup for NativeAOT and WebAssembly trimming compatibility.
   - `GraphIndex` and `TensorDataLoader` provide 1-hop producer/consumer navigation and on-demand zero-allocation weight slice reading.
   - `ModelParseException` (with `FileOffset` and `Context`) serves as the single exception boundary for malformed or truncated streams.
3. **Freeze the Manifest & Golden Topology Snapshot Schema (`v1`):**
   `models.manifest.json` (`schemaVersion: 1`) and `*.expected.json` snapshots produced by `SnapshotBuilder` and `DotNodeViewer.Cli snapshot` are frozen for regression testing across all subsequent format stages.

## Consequences

- Format stages (`S02`, `S03`, `S07`, `S08`, `S09`, `S10`) implement `internal` detectors and readers under `DotNodeViewer.Core.Formats.<Format>` and register them in `ModelFormatRegistry.Default` without altering public IR signatures.
- `PublicApiEncapsulationTests` enforces at test time that no `*.Formats.*`, `*.IO.*`, or third-party schema types leak into public signatures and that all exported reference types are `sealed` or closed discriminated unions.
- Any future change to `DotNodeViewer.Core.Ir`, `DotNodeViewer.Core.Abstractions`, or the snapshot schema requires recording a new superseding ADR.
